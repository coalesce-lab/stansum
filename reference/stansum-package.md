# stansum: Bayesian Models for Aggregated Relational Data and Network Scale-Up Method

Interface to various precompiled Stan models for Aggregated Relational
Data that estimate, among other things, network degrees. Includes models
by Zheng et al (2006)
[doi:10.1198/016214505000001168](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214505000001168)
, Maltiel et al (2015)
[doi:10.1214/15-AOAS827](https://doi.org/10.1214/15-AOAS827) , Baum &
Marsden (2023)
[doi:10.1016/j.socnet.2023.02.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socnet.2023.02.001)
.
